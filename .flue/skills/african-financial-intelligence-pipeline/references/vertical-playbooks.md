## Split Verticals

Use these verticals for scanning and classification:

1. Monetary policy, rates, inflation, FX, reserves
2. Banking regulation, supervision, enforcement, recapitalisation
3. Bank performance, earnings, capital adequacy, impairments
4. Capital markets, securities, IPOs, bonds, listings, delistings
5. Capital flows, DFI activity, PE/VC, debt funding, trade finance
6. Payments infrastructure, settlement, switches, mobile money, cards
7. Digital identity, KYC, fraud, cybersecurity, financial crime
8. Open banking, APIs, AI, core banking, operational technology
9. Fintech strategy, licences, partnerships, expansion, M&A
10. Human behaviour and market signals

## 1. Monetary Policy, Rates, Inflation, FX, Reserves

Search for central bank decisions, MPC communiques, inflation releases, exchange-rate reforms, reserve changes, liquidity tools, OMO activity, cash reserve requirements, and lending transmission.

Answer:

- What changed in the policy stance?
- What data justified the move?
- How does it affect banks, borrowers, investors, and FX liquidity?
- Is transmission likely to work, or is there a banking-system bottleneck?

## 2. Banking Regulation and Supervision

Search for circulars, recapitalisation rules, licence changes, sanctions, stress-test signals, corporate governance rules, HoldCo changes, cybersecurity rules, and compliance deadlines.

Answer:

- Who must act differently?
- What is the deadline or penalty?
- Which institutions are advantaged or pressured?
- Does it affect consolidation, capital raising, risk appetite, or product design?

## 3. Bank Performance and Competitor Activity

Search for results, annual reports, exchange filings, analyst notes, share-price moves, strategic updates, impairments, NPLs, NIM, capital adequacy, cost-to-income ratio, expansion, acquisition, rights issue, IPO, delisting, or partnership.

Answer:

- What changed financially or strategically?
- Is growth quality improving or deteriorating?
- Are profits driven by core lending, FX gains, fees, cost control, or one-offs?
- What does this reveal about the competitive position?

## 4. Capital Markets

Search for exchange announcements, new listings, equity market reforms, retail access, bond issuance, commercial paper, sukuk, Eurobonds, market-maker changes, trading rules, custody, settlement, and depository reforms.

Answer:

- Does this deepen liquidity or just add a headline transaction?
- Who are the issuers, arrangers, investors, and affected market participants?
- What does pricing signal about sovereign, bank, or corporate risk?
- Does market infrastructure change access for retail or institutional investors?

## 5. Capital Flows and Funding

Search DFI, PE, VC, bank lending, guarantees, trade finance, correspondent banking, and credit facility announcements.

Answer:

- Who is receiving money?
- Where is it coming from?
- What terms, tenor, currency, guarantee, or mandate are disclosed?
- What market gap is the capital trying to solve?
- Does it signal confidence, distress, strategic expansion, or risk mitigation?

## 6. Payments Infrastructure

Search switches, mobile money, cards, settlement systems, interoperability, instant payments, agent networks, PoS regulation, chargebacks, QR, contactless, direct debit, and cross-border remittances.

Answer:

- Which rail is changing?
- Does the change affect cost, speed, reliability, fraud risk, or market access?
- Who controls the distribution layer?
- What second-order effects appear for banks, fintechs, merchants, and consumers?

## 7. Identity, Fraud, Cybersecurity, Financial Crime

Search KYC rules, BVN/NIN/national ID integrations, cybersecurity incidents, fraud losses, APP fraud, AML/CFT enforcement, FATF changes, suspicious transaction reporting, and account-opening rules.

Answer:

- What loophole or risk is being addressed?
- Which institutions carry new liability?
- What evidence supports the fraud or cyber claim?
- Are consumer protection and institutional liability changing?

## 8. Open Banking, AI, APIs, Core Infrastructure

Search for approved pilots, API standards, open banking frameworks, bank technology adoption, core banking replacement, AI operational use cases, cloud infrastructure, and regulatory sandboxes.

Answer:

- Is there real institutional adoption or just vendor marketing?
- What operational process changes?
- What integration, compliance, cost, or data dependency matters?
- Which institutions are likely to benefit first?

## 9. Fintech Strategy, Licences, Partnerships, Expansion, M&A

Search for licence applications, acquisitions, geographic expansion, strategic partnerships, embedded finance moves, remittance corridors, banking-as-a-service, and material product shifts.

Answer:

- Is this a strategic shift or a promotional launch?
- What licence, partner, market access, or compliance requirement makes it possible?
- What does it cost in credibility, capital, talent, or operations?
- Does it change the competitive map?

## 10. Human Behaviour and Market Signals

Search professional commentary, user complaints, transaction workarounds, informal merchant practices, adoption gaps, fee sensitivity, downtime complaints, and recurring operational pain.

Answer:

- Is the behaviour repeated across multiple credible voices?
- What institutional data can corroborate it?
- Does it reveal a product gap, risk, pricing issue, trust issue, or opportunity?
- Is it safe to publish, or should it remain an internal lead?
